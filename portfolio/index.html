About Me

A blend of clean technical coding and digital creativity.

I am a web developer based in Bangladesh driven by curiosity and a desire to build things that make a positive impact online. My journey into web technology started with an interest in understanding how websites function behind the scenes — from early markup structure to responsive stylesheets and dynamic logic.

My focus centers on clean frontend development alongside solid backend fundamentals. I believe that writing organized, readable, and accessible code is just as vital as delivering aesthetic visual results.

In addition to writing code, I have hands-on experience in UI/UX wireframing and prototyping using Figma, as well as digital video production. This unique combination allows me to approach problems with an eye for user experience, design fidelity, and functional reliability.

I am continuously working on personal and real-world projects to refine my craft and prepare for a rewarding role as a Junior Web Developer or Full-Stack contributor.
